About Velvet Vogue

Going by the name Velvet Vogue, it is a fashion brand created for young adults who have a belief that fashion is a means of self-expression. We started in 2020 with the mission to combine trendy casuals and elegant formals in an affordable price range.

We're dedicated to ensuring you wear a piece that will make you feel confident and look your best, from everyday shirts to stand out evening gowns.

Our Values

Quality

We make sure our clothes are made from good fabrics so they don't tear easily and last longer in your wardrobe.

Style

We always look at the newest trends around the world so you can wear what's popular and stylish right now.

Customer First

We care about our buyers and want to make sure you have a really good and easy shopping experience with us.
